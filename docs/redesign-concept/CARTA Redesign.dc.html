The redesign · a note

The lot moved to the centre.

Principles

1The lot is the hero; everything else earns its place around it
2Typography is the interface — hierarchy by size, weight, case, space
3One ember per screen — the action or the score, never both decoration
4Never print a number that lied — unread beats invented
5Surface calm, depth one gesture away

What changed

NavigationToday · Atlas · Record · More — Home and Café become channels, not rooms; the instrument rides off Today
Findretired into the Atlas — search, the chart, the drawn plot
Reachreborn as the standing, on the coffee
The column480 px kept — one hand, one thumb, the ledger's measure

The voice

IdentityThe reference record of specialty coffee — one green traced through every hand, your taste the wash over it. We keep the record; we never make the sale.
The gate — every new line is read aloud against it.
Sentence casebody, buttons, labels — headings are uppercased by CSS, written lower in source
Tersefragments welcome; the em-dash over the conjunction
Own the refusalstate it plainly, name the reason, never bark
Address the readernot the market — no growth-speak, no exclamation
Never a number that liedno invented precision, date or score; unread is a state
Typographic numbersen-dash ranges, m:ss, n= counts, °C / °F
No emojimeaning by typographic glyph, never an icon or a party mark
The seal, sparinglyit closes a thing; it never garnishes one
One line per class, verbatim from a live surface — so this deck and the app can't drift.

Motion

Fast · 130mscontrols touched most — a chip, a dial: barely moves
Base · 230msa screen settles, a sheet rises — the standard physical move
Considered · 820msthe one sanctioned moment — arrival, the interlude
The curveone shared ease-out; nothing bounces, nothing pulses
The ruleused more, moves less — the more often a control is touched, the less it animates
Reduced motionfirst-class — the OS setting stills the interlude and the sheets to their end frame; every screen stays legible
Motion tokens are the one sanctioned break from inline styles — declared once as custom properties beside the type resets, referenced everywhere the interface moves.
